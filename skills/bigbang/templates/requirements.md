# Requirements

Owner-owned and append-only. `R<n>` blocks hold the owner's words verbatim, written only by `bigbang goals`.
`R<n>.<m> · <date> · refines R<n>` holds one reading of words that leave a choice open (the reading with fewer
features, and its source). `D<n> · <date> · <area> · req: R<n>` holds a decision that changes the layout.

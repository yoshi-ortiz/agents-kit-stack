compiler path, and how is reviewed intent delivered to that runner?

## Proposal to evaluate

Use its current compiler/runner where possible; declare exact or estimated
counting explicitly instead of introducing Google ADK as a prerequisite.

## Read when claimed

Compare mandatory overflow, unreviewed intent and scoped proof exceptions.

## Resolution evidence

The answer names owner/files, mandatory input shape, review evidence, tokenizer
version/counting mode, budget and runner interception point. No whole-catalog
migration is required. Resolve through a live decision.
